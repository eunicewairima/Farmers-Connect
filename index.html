How it works

Request produce

List your produce, quantities, and preferred delivery date. Keep your request reference for follow-up.

We review availability

We compare crop, available quantity, harvest date, and location. We contact farmers to confirm their details and stock before making an offer.

Review the offer

An offer should show the farmer or farm, available quantity, price per kilogram, delivery cost, and proposed delivery date. Review those details before accepting.
